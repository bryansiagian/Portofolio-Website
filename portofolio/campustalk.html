Overview

What is CampusTalk?

CampusTalk is a closed social platform built exclusively for the Institut Teknologi Del community. The idea came from a recurring problem: important campus communications — announcements from student affairs, class updates from lecturers, peer discussions among students — were scattered across WhatsApp groups, email threads, and notice boards, with no single place that everyone actually checked.

The platform brings together students, teaching assistants, lecturers, and administrative staff under one roof, each with access controls appropriate to their role. Students can post, reply, and follow threads. Lecturers and TAs can push announcements to their classes. Student affairs can broadcast campus-wide notices that don't get buried in a 500-person WhatsApp group.

Under the hood, a Flutter mobile app connects to a Laravel REST API with a PostgreSQL backend — chosen for its relational integrity on a data model with multiple user types, permissions, and thread relationships. All features, from student discussions to admin broadcasts, are handled entirely within the mobile experience.

Problem & Solution

The communication gap CampusTalk closes.

⚠️
The Problem
  • Campus communication is fragmented across WhatsApp groups, email, and physical notice boards. Critical announcements get buried or missed entirely.
  • WhatsApp groups have no structure — a 500-person class group mixes urgent notices with memes and off-topic chatter, making it unreliable for official communication.
  • There's no persistent record. Once a message scrolls out of a WhatsApp group, it's effectively gone. Students can't reference old announcements or decisions.
  • No role-based access anywhere. A student can't send a broadcast to their class, but a lecturer can't limit who replies to their announcements either.
✦
Our Approach
  • Built a role-aware platform where every user has permissions matching their position — students engage, TAs moderate, lecturers announce, staff broadcast.
  • Threaded discussions keep conversations organized by topic. Replies nest under the original post, and pins surface the most important content.
  • All posts are persistent and searchable. An announcement from three months ago is one search away — no more asking "did anyone screenshot that?"
  • REST API backend cleanly separates data from presentation, serving the Flutter mobile app seamlessly from a single, reliable source of truth.

Features

What the platform does.

📣
Role-Based Announcements
Lecturers and staff can push announcements scoped to a specific class, department, or the entire campus. Students see only what's relevant to them.
💬
Threaded Discussions
Posts spawn organized reply threads. Students can ask follow-up questions, TAs can answer, and the whole conversation stays in context — not scattered across replies.
📌
Pinned Posts
Lecturers and staff can pin important announcements so they stay visible at the top of the feed, regardless of how much new content comes in.
🔔
Push Notifications
Users get notified when there's a new announcement in their scope, a reply to their thread, or a campus-wide broadcast from student affairs.
🔐
Campus-Only Access
Authentication is scoped to IT Del. Only verified campus community members can join, keeping the platform closed, trusted, and free of external noise.
🔎
Persistent Search
Every post, announcement, and discussion is stored and searchable. No more asking a friend to resend something that was posted months ago.
